Tesla, Inc. (TSLA) — Company Overview

Tesla, Inc. designs, develops, manufactures, leases, and sells electric vehicles, energy generation and storage systems, and related services. Vehicle models include Model 3, Model Y, Model S, Model X, Cybertruck, and Semi. Energy products include Powerwall, Powerpack, and Megapack.

Financial highlights (FY2024):
- Revenue: ~$97 billion
- Net income: ~$7 billion
- Automotive gross margin: ~17% (declining from 2022 highs)
- Energy generation and storage revenue: ~$10 billion
- EPS (diluted): ~$2.04

Growth drivers:
- Full Self-Driving (FSD) technology monetization potential
- Robotaxi service launch anticipated in 2025
- Optimus humanoid robot in limited production
- Energy storage (Megapack) backlog growing rapidly
- New lower-cost vehicle (Model Q) planned for 2025

Key risks:
- Increasing competition from BYD and Chinese EV makers globally
- CEO Elon Musk political controversies affecting brand perception in key markets
- Margin compression from price cuts and legacy product cycle
- FSD regulatory approval timeline uncertainty
- High valuation relative to current earnings (P/E ~80x)

Recent news themes:
- Vehicle deliveries declining year-over-year amid competition and brand headwinds
- Cybertruck production ramping but facing quality recall issues
- Energy storage segment emerging as strong growth driver
- Robotaxi and autonomous driving technology updates drawing investor focus
