Apple Inc. (AAPL) — Company Overview

Apple Inc. designs, manufactures, and markets smartphones, personal computers, tablets, wearables, and accessories worldwide. Its flagship products include the iPhone, Mac, iPad, Apple Watch, and AirPods. The company also operates a Services segment generating revenue from the App Store, Apple Music, iCloud, Apple Pay, and AppleCare.

Financial highlights (FY2024):
- Revenue: ~$391 billion
- Net income: ~$94 billion
- Gross margin: ~46%
- Operating margin: ~31%
- EPS (diluted): ~$6.11
- P/E ratio: approximately 30x trailing earnings

Growth drivers:
- Services segment growing at double-digit rates, now exceeding $90 billion annually
- iPhone 15 Pro and iPhone 16 series driving upgrade cycles
- Expansion of Apple Intelligence (on-device AI features)
- India manufacturing ramp-up reducing geopolitical supply risk

Key risks:
- China revenue concentration (~18% of total revenue); tariff and regulatory exposure
- Smartphone market saturation in developed markets
- Antitrust regulatory pressure on App Store commissions (EU Digital Markets Act)
- Dependence on TSMC for advanced chip manufacturing

Recent news themes:
- Apple Intelligence rollout accelerating across iPhone, Mac, and iPad
- EU compliance changes for App Store in Europe
- Record share buyback program continuing
- Supply chain diversification to India and Vietnam
