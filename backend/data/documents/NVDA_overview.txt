NVIDIA Corporation (NVDA) — Company Overview

NVIDIA Corporation designs and markets graphics processing units (GPUs), system-on-chip units, and related software. It operates in two segments: Graphics and Compute & Networking. The company is the dominant supplier of AI training and inference chips through its H100 and B100/B200 Blackwell GPU families.

Financial highlights (FY2025):
- Revenue: ~$130 billion (full-year)
- Data Center revenue: ~$115 billion (fastest-growing segment)
- Net income: ~$72 billion
- Gross margin: ~75%
- Operating margin: ~60%
- EPS (diluted): ~$2.94 (adjusted)

Growth drivers:
- Explosive demand for AI training infrastructure from hyperscalers (Microsoft, Google, Amazon, Meta)
- Blackwell GPU architecture ramping to meet record backlog
- CUDA software ecosystem creating strong switching costs
- Networking (InfiniBand/Ethernet) segment growing alongside compute
- Sovereign AI: national governments building domestic AI infrastructure

Key risks:
- US export controls on advanced chips to China (~$15B potential revenue risk)
- AMD and Intel competing in AI accelerator market
- Customer concentration: top 5 hyperscaler customers represent majority of Data Center revenue
- Cyclical semiconductor industry; inventory corrections possible

Recent news themes:
- Blackwell GPU production ramp beating expectations
- US government tightening export control rules on H20 chip to China
- NVIDIA expanding into AI agents and robotics with Isaac platform
- Partnerships with major cloud providers for dedicated GPU clusters
